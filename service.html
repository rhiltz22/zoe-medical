How the repair process works

Repairs are diagnosed and completed in-house. Here's what to expect after you submit the form above.

  1. 1 Submit this form with your serial number and a description of the problem.
  2. 2 We'll issue a Return Material Authorization (RMA) and shipping instructions.
  3. 3 Send the unit in — we diagnose, repair, and return it. For out-of-warranty repairs we provide a quotation before proceeding.
