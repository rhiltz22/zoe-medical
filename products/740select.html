Technical specifications

Display 7" color touchscreen, 800×480
Battery life Up to 8 hours continuous use
Weight 2.8 lbs (1.27 kg) with battery
Standard parameters SpO2, NIBP, 3-lead ECG, Temp
Optional parameters 12-lead ECG, ETCO2, IBP
Connectivity Bluetooth, USB-C, optional Wi-Fi
Operating temp 0°C – 50°C (32°F – 122°F)
Storage temp -20°C – 60°C
IP rating IP44 splash resistant
Warranty 2 years parts and labor

Key features

Configurable parameter sets

Spec only the parameters your service requires — keeps cost down and workflow simple. Add parameters later as your needs change.

Hot-swappable battery

Swap batteries in the field without powering down. No interruption to patient monitoring during battery changes.

US-based service and support

All service and repairs handled in Topsfield, MA by the same team that built the product. Typical turnaround under 5 business days.

Compatible with Nightingale central monitoring

Feeds live data to the Nightingale MPC nurse station system for fleet-wide alarm surveillance and trending.
