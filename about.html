Our story

Built for clinicians, by engineers who care

Most of our early work was designing and building monitoring products for other companies — including some of the world's most recognized healthcare organizations. That deep OEM experience gave us insight into what clinicians actually need from a monitor: reliability, simplicity, and a manufacturer that answers the phone.

Today we distribute monitors under our own Zoe Medical and Nightingale labels at factory-direct prices, passing the savings from cutting out distributors directly to our customers. Same engineering, no middlemen.

Company timeline
  1. 1996

    Founded in Topsfield, MA as a contract medical device design and engineering firm.

  2. Early 2000s

    Expanded into full manufacturing. First OEM contracts with major international healthcare brands.

  3. 2010s

    Launched the 740SELECT and Nightingale product lines under the Zoe Medical label. First direct-to-market sales.

  4. Today

    Tens of thousands of units installed across EMS, physician offices, and surgical settings worldwide.

What we stand for

Our principles

Clinical reliability

Every product is built to perform in the moments that matter most. We design and test to clinical-grade standards because there's no acceptable failure rate when a patient is on the monitor.

Factory-direct value

No distributors, no middlemen, no distributor markup. We sell direct so clinicians get quality monitoring at a price that makes sense for their budget — without sacrificing capability.

Made in America

Designed, manufactured, and supported in Topsfield, MA. We believe in domestic production and standing behind what we make — with the team that built it, not a third-party service center.
