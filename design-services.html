Design services

License our platform. Build under your own name.

For 30 years, Zoe Medical has designed and built patient monitoring products for other companies — including some of the most respected names in the field. Put that engineering to work for your product.

The value of a stable platform

A design platform built to extend

Zoe Medical combines a broad suite of Microsoft technologies with proprietary signal processing circuitry, algorithms, networking, and user interface applications to create our patient monitoring solutions. This approach leverages the rich feature set of the Windows and Azure Cloud environments to create a design platform that is extendable.

Using this platform, we provide design services to companies who wish to sell a customized version of our products under their own name, or who wish to use our design as the backbone for a new product.

Based on your need, we can provide sub-assemblies, turn-key systems, or a platform upon which your own application can run.
